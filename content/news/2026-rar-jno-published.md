<section class="feature-section">
  <h2>What We Found</h2>
  <p>Using wrist actigraphy and DNA methylation data from adults in the Baltimore Epidemiologic Catchment Area Study, we found that stronger and more stable rest-activity rhythms were associated with lower GrimAge and PhenoAge acceleration. Greater rhythm fragmentation and more variable activity timing were associated with higher acceleration.</p>
</section>
