Presented a poster at SLEEP 2026 on [Long-Term Insomnia Burden and Inflammaging and Immunosenescence in Older Adults](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article/49/Supplement_1/A52/8673852).

Using repeated insomnia symptom measures and blood-based biomarkers from the Health and Retirement Study, this work examines whether chronic insomnia burden is associated with markers of inflammaging and adaptive immunosenescence in older adults.

The abstract was published in the *Sleep* supplement as poster 0120, page A52.
